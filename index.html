How a conversation works

Most routine questions end at step one. The rest move to a person without starting over.

  1. The AI answers

    Customers chat with a Claude-powered assistant that replies from your knowledge base and handles routine questions instantly.

  2. It hands off when needed

    If the issue isn't resolved, the conversation moves to a live agent with the full chat and a short summary.

  3. An agent finishes the job

    The customer continues with the agent over chat or a video call, so complex or sensitive cases get a human.

Built to hand over, not hide behind

An AI assistant is only useful if customers can reach a person when it falls short. Virtual Customer Support is designed around that handover: the assistant answers what it can, says when it can't, and passes the full context to an agent.

Answers are grounded in the content you provide, so the assistant stays within what your business actually offers.

Questions

Short answers to the things businesses ask first.

What powers the AI assistant?

The assistant is built on Claude from Anthropic. It answers using the knowledge base and policies you provide.

What happens when the AI can't answer?

The conversation is handed to a live agent with the full chat and a summary. The customer can continue by chat or on a video call.

Do customers need to install anything for video calls?

Video calls are powered by Cloudflare RealtimeKit. Ask us for a demo to see the exact customer experience.

Can I use my own human agents?

Yes. The platform is designed so your own support staff take over escalated conversations.
